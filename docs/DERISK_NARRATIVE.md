## Every change, by release

### Release 0.1.0

**`sw_central_expectation` — F10.7 central expectation at a lead, version 2** · Q3-26 · 2026-09-15 · math, model

- *What we believed:* That the record's unconditional mean was the right centre — a mission through solar minimum and one through maximum designed to the same flux.
- *What we tested:* The record by cycle phase: its F10.7 runs 64 to 343 sfu, almost entirely by where in the cycle it is. Two completed cycles aligned on their maxima and normalised by their own 81-day peaks; the window average compared with what the two cycles ran over the same span past maximum; twelve fixtures watched to fail on a 1e-6 shape perturbation, a dropped epoch and a broken modulo fold.
- *What we now know:* The constant was 26 per cent high for a five-year mission opening in 2027 — the record ran at 91.2 sfu over that span, scaled onto cycle 25. The unconditional mean is right only once the window spans more than a cycle.
- *What it cost:* one commit; no closure flipped
- *What changed:* The centre is now the cycle analogue averaged over the mission's own dates. At the declared epoch it moves 114.84 to 90.39 sfu, and the design value and four downstream rows follow it down by 24.46 sfu; the l3_solar_req_01/02 margin widens from 21.9 to 46.3. It degrades into the old answer exactly where the old one was right: 119.10 sfu at fifteen years.
- *Rests on now:* Cycle shape repeats between cycles, and every cycle is the size of cycle 25 — its 81-day peak of 225.1 sfu.
- *Would break if:* A mission window runs past one period beyond cycle 25's maximum, into a cycle whose size is not cycle 25's.
- *Risks:* R-01 L4->L3

**`sw_central_expectation` — F10.7 central expectation at a lead, version 3** · Q3-26 · 2026-09-15 · math, algorithm

- *What we believed:* That past one period the analogue could keep scaling by cycle 25's own peak — that the next cycle is this one's size — and that a 45-day grid was fine enough to hold the shape.
- *What we tested:* The wrap and the amplitude, read against the completed cycles' own peaks (cycle 23 226.8 sfu, cycle 24 160.9); the grid against the 4178-day period; three mutations watched to fail — a dropped handover limit, a removed amplitude handover (11 fixtures) and a single-fold wrap (2).
- *What we now know:* Cycles repeat their shape and not their size, so beyond cycle 25 the amplitude was a guess; and the 45-day grid did not divide the period, which left a 1.2 sfu discontinuity at the wrap that no reading of the numbers would have found.
- *What it cost:* one commit; no closure flipped
- *What changed:* Outside cycle 25 the amplitude is the mean of the completed cycles' peaks, 193.9 sfu; the grid is 94 knots of 44.4468 days, so the handover falls on a knot. At the declared epoch the centre moves 90.39 to 86.85 sfu and the design value 203.68 to 200.14. The window's highest stretch became its own row, sw_window_peak_level.
- *Rests on now:* Cycle shape repeats between cycles, measured on two completed cycles; outside cycle 25 a cycle's size is the mean of the last two peaks.
- *Would break if:* Cycle 26's peak departs far from 193.9 sfu, or a third completed cycle's shape disagrees with the two-cycle analogue — the whole sample is two cycles, and eleven of ninety-three shape points rest on one of them.
- *Risks:* R-01 L3->L2
