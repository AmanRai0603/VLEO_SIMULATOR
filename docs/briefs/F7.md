>
> **Kind:** brief · **For:** Person B, S6 (Saturday 17 October, night)

Plan: `docs/PLAN_F.md`, F7. The operating model: W9, W10; section 15.

## Builds

1. **The design pointer (high risk).** Each released design is written whole
   under its own name; the file that says which is current is replaced by one
   rename. A reader that finds a half-written release ignores it and says so.
2. **The Programme workspace:** mission health (each customer's KPIs closed
   or not, with margins; cost against target), gates, risks from the
   de-risking record, the organisation.
3. **A decision** signed into a release of the programme's branch, and taken
   into today's design at once (W10).
4. **The released design beside today's** in every workspace, for each level's
   review (W9).

## Proved by

- A release interrupted half-way: the previous design stays current.
- A design released, reviewed at each level, and a programme decision taken
  back into today's design.
