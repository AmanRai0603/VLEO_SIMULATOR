# F5 · System, at a subsystem's valve

> **Answer first.** A subsystem engineer frames their group, assigns its nodes, sees today's view and the impact of their changes, seals a release that records its base, and answers values another group moves. B builds it on S4; the seal is high risk, reviewed by A with Opus on Saturday morning and approved by Aman.
>
> **Kind:** brief · **For:** Person B, S4 (Friday 16 October, night)

Plan: `docs/PLAN_F.md`, F5. The operating model: section 4 (System
workspace), W2, W4, W5, W7, W11 (assign), W12.

## Starts from

- `vleo_files::seal` (the seal rules) and `chain::check_release`.
- F1 (who owns the group), F3 (signed nodes), F4 (the branch as a tree).

## Builds

1. **Frame:** wire, assign and issue node files; the sheet that sets up many
   nodes at once.
2. **Today's view of the branch,** built from every node engineer's latest
   signed revision, inside today's design; **impact:** every value outside the
   branch that its changes move.
3. **The seal (high risk):** every node signed at its current contract,
   nothing behind, no duplicate files, the group's checks passing; the release
   records its base (`base_version`, F1) and is written into `releases/`.
4. **Answers:** a subsystem engineer named by another group's release answers
   *fine* or *object, because…*, signed (W7).
5. **The base update** when a newer design is released (W12); **a contract
   change** leaving the nodes that read it *behind* until re-signed (W4).
6. **Issues assigned,** and closed by the release whose notes name them (W11).
7. **Removed:** the group and node pages, and `xtask group-app` (decision 1).

## Proved by

- A sealed release appearing in today's design for a second person (W5).
- A contract change leaving its readers behind until re-signed.
- An objection answered.
- A seal refused, by name, while one node is unsigned.
