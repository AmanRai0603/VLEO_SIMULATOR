## F6-a · the frame and start group (planned 17 October)

Planned from the code on the stack that holds F1 to F4 and F3 (to
`claude/a-f3h3-removal`), before F5's seal: items 1 and 2 above do not wait on
it. **High risk:** it signs the systems' file, and writes and signs a new
group file. Opus review, Aman approves. Nothing is computed: no margin, no
budget, no answer moves.

### What it starts from, found while planning

- Who owns a group is read from the file that mounts it (`chain::mounted_by`,
  `owner_of`): the mount, then the first assignment on its block. A group file
  opens only signed by that owner or their deputy (`open_group_in`), and
  registers only node engineers.
- Only the programme's and the systems' files mount (59cb6f64: a group file
  that mounts another is not its parent). F6-a keeps that rule.
- F4-a's pipeline (`breakdown::edit`, `edit_in_place`) already opens through
  the chain, asks `may_frame`, a why, writes a change row, signs afresh, opens
  again, under the copy's lock, and stops a second writer. Its `apply`
  refuses a block that carries a mount ("a mount is changed in F6").
- An allocation today is a port whose `state` is `allocated`;
  which way it binds lives only in a `closure` row's `sense`, which nothing in
  the one schema reads yet.
- Group files stay in the copy: put-back takes none back, and today's design
  takes only releases from before 1.0.

### Builds

1. **Mount** (`breakdown::Edit::Mount`, applied by a new `frame.rs`), in the
   systems' file only, by the system engineer or their deputy: a `mount` row
   and its owner's `assignment` on a leaf nobody writes, the block's behaviour
   `children`, a change row, signed afresh, opened again. W13 too: the block
   may be a leaf of a subsystem's branch, and that branch's owner becomes the
   **valve above** the new group (`frame::valve_above`). Refused by name, with
   nothing written: an id that is not one, `programme` or `systems`, a group
   mounted already, a folder that holds a group file already, a block no
   branch holds, one with children, broken down, assigned, archived or in the
   programme's branch, and an owner who is not registered as a subsystem
   engineer or the system engineer, or is someone's deputy.
2. **Allocate** (`Edit::Allocate`), on a mount framed in the systems' file: an
   `in` port, state `allocated`, parameter `system`, a value or a range, and
   its `closure` with its `sense`, never defaulted. Refused without a sense,
   with a sense that is not `<=` or `>=`, bounding an output the block does
   not have, and on an issued node, whose ports are its contract (W4).
3. **Start group** (`start::start_in_place`), by the owner the mount names, or
   their deputy: `groups/<g>/<g>.vgroup`, a new file, with its meta, its base
   copied from the systems' file, one top heading (its question copied from
   the mount), a change row, signed by the owner, opened through
   `open_group_in`. Refused for anyone else, for a group nobody mounts, one
   started already, a folder that holds other files, and a systems' file that
   does not open.
4. **F4's edits learn** that a block any file mounts is not theirs to break
   down, remove or move (`frame::mounted_on_any`, read by `breakdown::apply`
   and put-back).
5. **The routes:** `POST /v1/branch/mount`, `POST /v1/branch/allocate`,
   `POST /v1/group/start`; `GET /v1/branch` gains the valve above, the
   allocations, the mounts and whether the open key may mount; a group
   mounted and not started is said as that, with its next step. My work gains
   *Start <g>* for its owner.
6. **The System workspace:** the valve above and below; *mount a group here*
   on a leaf for the system engineer; *allocate* on a mount, with a sense
   chosen, never defaulted; *Start <g>* for its owner; budgets and margins as
   a link to Explore's health map, said as the engine's.
