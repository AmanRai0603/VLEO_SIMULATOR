sw_ap_design · computed · solar · owner environment · published

This is a form for one node of the design, and it is the whole of it — it needs no connection and nothing installed. Change what should change, say who you are, and save a filled copy. Send the saved file to the developer team: they check it, apply it to the node, and the next release of the tool carries your change, where you can run it with your own inputs.

  1. Fill in what you know. Each question says why it is asked; a question you cannot answer, leave as it is.
  2. Say who filled it, and whether an assistant helped. If one helped with the relation itself — the equation, its steps or its derivation — say so: those are then derived by a developer rather than taken from the form.
  3. Known-good values — an answer you know at given inputs, and where it comes from — go under known values. They are recorded by a person, with their source, and never guessed.
  4. Press save a filled copy and send that file. You can reopen it and carry on.

To fill it with an assistant, give it this file and ask it to edit only the block marked vleo-node-form near the end: plain TOML, one line per answer.
