## Said simply

How many days a year the daily level of geomagnetic disturbance goes above the level the vehicle is built for, counted over 28.197 years of record. At the default storm grade G3 it is 0.284 days a year, so about 1.42 days over a five-year mission, which turns a broken requirement into a size a programme can decide on.

## Picture it

Think of a flood mark on a riverbank and a diary kept for 28 years. Count the days the water went above the mark and divide by the years. At storm grade G3 that is eight days in 28.197 years, 0.284 a year, about 1.42 days over a five-year mission.

## Where it breaks

It is a rate from the past, not a forecast, and at G3 it rests on only eight days; its honest range per mission is roughly 0.9 to 2.0 days. Between the three grade levels the straight-line reading overstates the rate badly, and the nine missing months of 2017 fall where exceedances cluster, so the true rate is more likely higher.

## Common misreading

It is tempting to treat the design level as a limit the sky respects. The record holds days above every storm grade below G4, so the choice is how many days of exceedance to accept, not whether there are any.
