## Said simply

The highest solar activity the design must survive on any single day inside the mission: 350 solar flux units, just above the record's worst day, 343 on 2023-02-17. Survive, not operate in: a day at this level may cost a safe mode and some propellant, but not the vehicle.

## Picture it

Think of a roof rated for a little more than the heaviest single snowfall on record. On that one day the road may close and someone may have to shovel, but the roof must stay up. This row is that rating for one day of solar activity: 350 against the record's worst day of 343.

## Where it breaks

It is anchored in 28.2 years of satellite-era record, not the Sun's whole history; cycle 19 in the late 1950s was reported above 350. It limits an index, not the density and heating the spacecraft feels.

## Common misreading

Checking the sustained achieved level against this single-day ceiling, or the reverse. The two are different commitments, and crossing them reports a margin that belongs to neither.
