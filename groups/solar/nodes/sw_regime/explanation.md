## In one line

Which of three states a day's geomagnetic activity is in, numbered 1, 2 and 3: quiet up to a daily index of 6, active up to 25, storm above.

## Said simply

Which of three states a day's geomagnetic activity is in, numbered 1, 2 and 3: quiet up to a daily index of 6, active up to 25, storm above. A design cannot react to every small change in the index, but it can keep a separate budget for each state.

## Where it breaks

The label says nothing about how deep into a state a day is: a day at 26 and a day at 200 are both storm. It carries no confidence, looks at nothing but today's index, and in any run today it can only say active or storm, because its only input is a design storm level between 20 and 230.

## Common misreading

That the number is a severity, as if storm were three times quiet. It is an index of which population the day belongs to; the active band is nineteen units of the daily index wide, while the step from active to storm is one.
