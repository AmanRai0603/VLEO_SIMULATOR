## Said simply

The highest sustained level of solar activity the design commits to operate in for the whole mission: 260 solar flux units, just above the hottest 27-day stretch in the record, 252.67 on 2024-08-13. The record's actual level for this mission is checked against it.

## Picture it

Picture a riverside town setting its flood wall just above the highest level the river has held for a month in living memory. The wall is the town's own capability, and each year's river is checked against it rather than the wall being moved to suit it. The wall also says nothing about whether the cellars stay dry, just as this ceiling says nothing about the air density the spacecraft feels.

## Where it breaks

It is a ceiling on an index of solar radio flux, not on what the spacecraft feels, which is the air density that activity produces. And the record behind it is the satellite era only: an earlier solar cycle, cycle 19 in the late 1950s, ran higher than anything in it.

## Common misreading

Expecting the requirement to follow the mission's dates. It does not move with the epoch; it is the vehicle's capability, and the sky's variation belongs on the achieved side.
