## Said simply

How much room is left between the geomagnetic storm the design must not be destroyed by and the storm the record expects once in a five-year mission. The geomagnetic index here measures how disturbed the Earth's magnetic field is on a day.

## Where it breaks

It carries no confidence level, because geomagnetic activity has no usable long-term forecast. The expected storm, 158.38, sits above the 132 the vehicle operates through, so a passing margin here does not mean the storm case is closed: the mission is still outside its operating range for about 1.42 days over five years.

## Common misreading

Treating this and the single-day geomagnetic closure as the same question. This one is a return level from the whole record, 158.4; the other is one scenario's worst day, 90.5.
