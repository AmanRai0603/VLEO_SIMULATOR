## In one line

How much room is left between the sustained solar activity the design is committed to operate in and the level the record says this mission's window will actually present.

## Said simply

How much room is left between the sustained solar activity the design is committed to operate in and the level the record says this mission's window will actually present. Positive means room to spare; negative means the requirement is exceeded, and by how much.

## Where it breaks

A passing margin says the sky is one the design says it can operate in, not that the design is adequate: the spacecraft feels the air density that solar activity produces, through a model this subsystem does not own. The level it reads is a 90th-percentile band, though the run is labelled 95 per cent.

## Common misreading

Taking this margin as proof the spacecraft copes. It only compares an index of solar activity against a ceiling on that index.
