## Picture it

Think of the calmest week of a quiet month, and then the stillest single day inside that week. You start from the quiet stretch and take off how far one day can dip below its own stretch. The result sits close to the floor, because there is not much room below a calm week.

## Where it breaks

Nothing in the arithmetic keeps the answer above zero: a sustained quiet level below about 15.6 would push it through the floor and the row would refuse. Stacking a quiet stretch with a quiet day inside it also makes something rarer than the 95 per cent the label suggests, since quiet days and quiet stretches are not independent.

## Common misreading

It is tempting to build the quiet day by mirroring the disturbed day. The upward swings are two to three times the downward ones and are read at different levels, so mirroring would give about minus 46, which is not a sky.
