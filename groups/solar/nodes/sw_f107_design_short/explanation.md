## Where it breaks

Adding two one-sided margins gives a day rarer than the 95 per cent label, roughly one in a hundred if they were independent, and they are not independent because both widen with activity. And a one-in-twenty day is routine over a long mission, about eighteen times in a year; the rare day a design may care about is further out and not published here.

## Common misreading

It is tempting to get the cold single day by mirroring this one. The two daily tails differ at every level, so the cold case is its own row.
