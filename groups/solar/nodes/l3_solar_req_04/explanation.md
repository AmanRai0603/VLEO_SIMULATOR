## Said simply

The sustained geomagnetic disturbance the design commits to keep operating in for the whole mission: a daily level of 48, the published threshold for a 'minor' storm. It means operating through minor-storm conditions indefinitely, with no safe mode.

## Picture it

Think of a fishing boat certified to work in moderate seas day after day, with no running for harbour. That everyday rating has to be lower than the rougher sea it can ride out once. This row is the everyday rating: a daily level of 48, the 'minor' storm threshold.

## Where it breaks

The number is a published threshold, which makes it checkable but not proven right: where a particular design must stop operating depends on its thermal, torque and propellant limits, which this subsystem does not see. It limits an index, not what the spacecraft feels.

## Common misreading

Reading a passing check here as proof the design is adequate. It only says the disturbance index stays under the committed level.
