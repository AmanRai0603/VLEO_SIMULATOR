Where in the solar cycle the days above the design level of geomagnetic disturbance tend to fall, as the middle value on a scale from zero at the cycle's start to one at its end. At the default storm grade G3 it is 0.603, just past the cycle's peak on the declining side; the mission's declared start sits at 0.619, inside that band.

## Picture it

Think of marking every stormy day on a clock face that goes round once per solar cycle. At G3 the marks fall between 0.294 and 0.736, and the middle one sits at 0.603, just past the peak. The storms come in a season about five years wide, not on a date.

## Where it breaks

It is a middle value with no spread beside it: at G3 the eight days run from 0.294 to 0.736, a band about five years wide. Eight days is a coarse sample, the phase scale is uncertain because the current cycle has not ended, and nine missing months of 2017 fall just past the edge of that band.

## Common misreading

It is easy to read 0.603 as when exceedances happen. It is where the middle one happened; a schedule needs the whole band, which is nearly half a cycle wide.
