## Said simply

How low the Sun's flux can be on a single day inside the mission window: the sustained cold level with a quiet day's own dip taken off. It comes to about 65.24 solar flux units, a genuinely quiet day just above the floor of 60, and it is the case a drag-based capability is weakest at.

## Picture it

Take a quiet stretch of weeks, and then the quietest day inside it. The stretch sits at about 69.63; a quiet day dips a further 4.39 below it, giving about 65.24. The Sun has a floor and no ceiling, so near the bottom there is little room left to fall.

## Where it breaks

Stacking a cold stretch with a cold day inside it gives a day rarer than the 95 per cent label suggests, nearer one in a hundred, and a quiet stretch is made of quiet days so they are not independent. The daily dip is matched to the level but not to where in the cycle that level occurs.

## Common misreading

It looks like the hot single day with the sign flipped. The two tails differ and are read at different levels, 69.63 here against 104.07 there, so mirroring would put this about sixteen units below what the record supports.
