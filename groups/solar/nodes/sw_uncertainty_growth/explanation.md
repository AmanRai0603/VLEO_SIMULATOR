## In one line

How much the Sun's flux can rise over a given lead time, at 95 per cent confidence, measured on the record.

## Said simply

How much the Sun's flux can rise over a given lead time, at 95 per cent confidence, measured on the record. It is added to the central expectation to make the design value, so the margin widens as the mission reaches further into a future nobody can see clearly.

## Where it breaks

It is the 95th percentile only; a mission needing 99 per cent confidence reads a number 32 flux units too small at a one-year lead. The longest leads rest on few independent points in the solar cycle, because 28 years of record hold barely two and a half cycles.

## Common misreading

That the uncertainty grows steadily with lead time. It rises to +114 at four years, falls to +66 at ten to eleven, and rises again to +115 at fifteen, because a lead of about a full cycle returns to a similar point in it.
