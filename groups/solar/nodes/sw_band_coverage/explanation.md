## Said simply

A count of whether the Sun's stated one-in-twenty upper band on its flux index really holds: how often, over 128135 pairs of days at seventeen lead times, the rise stayed inside the band. The answer is 0.9509, so the band is honest and very slightly on the safe side.

## Picture it

A forecaster promises the temperature will stay under a line on 95 days in 100. To check, you go back through the diary and count the days it did. Here the diary is the flux record, the line is the band at each of seventeen lead times, and the count over 128135 pairs of days comes back 0.9509.

## Where it breaks

It is checked on the same data the band was measured from, so it confirms the band was computed and wired correctly, not that it will hold in future. The pairs overlap heavily, so the real sample is far smaller than it looks, and it counts only rises above the band, not falls below it.

## Common misreading

It is tempting to read 0.9509 as proof the band will hold out of sample. It is close to arithmetic on the same pairs; its worth is that it could have come back wrong if the table had been built wrong.
