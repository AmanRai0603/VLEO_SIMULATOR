## Said simply

By how much the published solar flux outlook misses on average, with sign, at the far end of its window. It is low at every lead, by about 4 solar flux units at the far end, and low flux means thinner air, less drag and a longer predicted lifetime than the mission will fly: the unsafe direction.

## Where it breaks

It is one average over 29 years of a changing Sun, while a forecaster's miss on a busy day differs from a quiet one; a design at a known point in the cycle is owed a bias for that point, which this does not give. The miss also varies with lead in a non-straight way.

## Common misreading

A miss of about four units on a mean of 115 looks negligible. It is an offset under the whole margin, and a symmetric band around a biased centre is still in the wrong place; the fix is to add it back, not to widen the margin.
