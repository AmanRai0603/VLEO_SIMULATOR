## Said simply

The quiet level of geomagnetic disturbance the design must still work at, held over the mission window: the expected centre with a margin taken off the low side, about 17.5 on the daily index. A quiet field means the coolest, thinnest upper atmosphere, the weakest signal for a magnetometer and the least push for a magnetorquer, so quiet is a design case too.

## Picture it

A sailor plans for the windiest stretch of a passage, but also for the calmest, because a boat in a flat calm cannot steer. Here the magnetorquer is the sail: a quiet magnetic field gives it the least to push against. So the design needs a quiet case as well as a stormy one.

## Where it breaks

The margin is taken off symmetrically, but the index cannot go below zero, so at a centre near 4.6 or lower the arithmetic would cross zero and only a guard stops it. The margin is nearer a 90th-percentile edge than the 95 per cent the run is called, and one spread covers the whole window though it changes over the cycle.

## Common misreading

The quiet side is often treated as the harmless one. A quiet field sets the least torque a magnetorquer can produce, and that sizes an actuator.
