## Said simply

The ordinary level of geomagnetic disturbance the mission window is expected to sit at, measured on the daily index of how unsettled Earth's magnetic field is. Every other row of this kind gives an extreme; this one gives the centre that the design bands are built around, about 22.1.

## Picture it

Think of planning a long trip with only last month's average temperature in hand, and using it for every month you are away. It gives you a sensible middle to build a hot case and a cold case around. But it does not know the seasons are turning, and it is an average, not the temperature of any one day.

## Where it breaks

It is simply the last forecast before the record ends, held flat across the whole window. It carries no trend with the solar cycle, so a window opening next month and one opening years out get the same number, and it is an average over about 27 days, not the level of any one day.

## Common misreading

It is easy to read this as the level of a typical day. It is the mean of a 27-day stretch, so by construction about half the days in the window sit above it.
