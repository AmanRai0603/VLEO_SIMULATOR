## Where it breaks

It is one scenario's worst day, not the worst the whole record holds: the storm expected once in the mission is 158.4. Its daily term is read at the top of a table whose top rests on 300 days, and the band beneath it is the 90th percentile though labelled 95 per cent.

## Common misreading

Reading this and the storm closure as rival answers. This one belongs to the scenario, 90.5; the storm one belongs to the whole record, 158.4. A design needs both.
