## In one line

Which of four standard levels of solar activity a given daily reading of the Sun's radio brightness falls in: low, moderate, elevated or high, numbered one to four.

## Said simply

Which of four standard levels of solar activity a given daily reading of the Sun's radio brightness falls in: low, moderate, elevated or high, numbered one to four. A bare reading such as 228 means little to most readers; the level says it is the top one.

## Where it breaks

The level is a label carried as a number, so averaging levels or treating level four as twice level two is wrong: the levels are unequal in width. The top level has no upper edge, so it stops telling cases apart where the design cares most. And it labels a single day, while a mission passes through every level.

## Common misreading

It is easy to read the level of the design value as the level the mission will experience; it only says where the design point sits, since the record spends 39.8 per cent of its days in the lowest level and 12.8 per cent in the highest.
