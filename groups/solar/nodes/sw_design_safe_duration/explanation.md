## In one line

How long a mission could last before the record expects the chosen design level of geomagnetic disturbance to be exceeded once.

## Said simply

How long a mission could last before the record expects the chosen design level of geomagnetic disturbance to be exceeded once. At the default storm grade G3 it is 2.62 years, against a declared mission of five years, so the design is expected to be exceeded, and this says roughly when that becomes likely.

## Picture it

Think of a river that tops a flood mark once every 2.62 years on average. That does not mean a visit shorter than 2.62 years is safe: the flood can come next month or not for ten years. It is the length of stay at which you should expect, on average, one flood day.

## Where it breaks

It is not a countdown: a storm expected once in 2.62 years can come in the first month or not for ten years. It inherits a fit whose top end rests on only two storms; at G3 the answer sits well inside the fitted range, but a much higher design level would lean on that thin end.

## Common misreading

It is easy to read 2.62 years as a safe period before failure. It is where the expected count of such days passes one; over the five-year mission the expected count is 1.42 days.
