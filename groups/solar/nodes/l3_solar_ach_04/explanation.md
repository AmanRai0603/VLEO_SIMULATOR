## Said simply

How much room is left between the sustained geomagnetic disturbance the design is committed to operate in and what the record says this mission's window will present. Positive is room; negative is a violation and its size.

## Where it breaks

Both numbers are ceilings on an index; what a spacecraft feels is the heating, density and torque that disturbance produces, through models this subsystem does not own. The level it reads is a 90th-percentile band on a cycle-based estimate, though the run is labelled 95 per cent, and the residuals behind it are skewed.

## Common misreading

Reading this sustained level as the single-day or the storm value. The three are 26.70, 41.70 and 158.38, far enough apart that swapping them could turn a pass into a failure.
