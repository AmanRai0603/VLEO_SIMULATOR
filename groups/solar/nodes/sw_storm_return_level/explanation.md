## In one line

The daily geomagnetic activity the mission can expect to meet about once in its lifetime.

## Said simply

The daily geomagnetic activity the mission can expect to meet about once in its lifetime. There is no useful long-range storm forecast, so the design sizes for the worst storm it is likely to meet; a longer mission meets a worse one. At the declared five years it is about 158.4.

## Where it breaks

The curve rests on 28.2 years of record and its top end on two observations; the longest missions are a small extrapolation past the fitted range. Storms cluster over days, so there are fewer independent storms than storm days and the true uncertainty is wider than the fit suggests. A once-per-century storm needs a longer record.

## Common misreading

That it can answer for a storm rarer than the record. A return period beyond the record's 28.2 years is refused, and the answer sits below the record's largest single day, 273, because that day was deliberately left out of the fit.
