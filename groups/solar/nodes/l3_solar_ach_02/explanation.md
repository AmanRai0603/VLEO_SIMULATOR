## Where it breaks

The day it describes stacks a daily swing on top of a 90th-percentile sustained level, so it is rarer than its label suggests, nearer a one-in-a-hundred day than one-in-twenty. And it is a ceiling on an index, not on the density and heating the spacecraft feels.

## Common misreading

Reading the single-day margin as the sustained one, or the other way round. The sheet's three solar-flux levels, 124.14, 104.07 and 200.14, share a unit, so nothing in the tree would catch the swap.
