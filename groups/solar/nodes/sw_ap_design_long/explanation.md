## Said simply

The disturbed level of geomagnetic activity the design must survive, held over the mission window: the expected centre plus a margin, about 26.7 on the daily index. It feeds density, then drag, then lifetime, and is what a propellant budget integrates.

## Picture it

Think of packing for a long trip. You do not pack for the usual weather alone; you pack for the usual weather plus a bit, because forecasts miss. Here the usual level of geomagnetic activity over the window is about 22.1, the forecast has typically missed by about 3.59, and 1.28 of those misses on top gives about 26.7.

## Where it breaks

The margin of 1.28 spreads is really a 90th-percentile edge while the run is labelled 95 per cent. The index cannot go below zero and swings a long way upward, so a symmetric margin understates the far upper tail, although at this particular margin it slightly over-covers.

## Common misreading

This is not the same as the storm level the design must survive once per mission; that asks what extreme recurs, this asks what level the window sits at, and the two differ by a factor of several.
