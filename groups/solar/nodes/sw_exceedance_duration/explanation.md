## Said simply

Once the daily level of geomagnetic disturbance goes above the level the vehicle is built for, how many days in a row it stays there. At the default storm grade G3 it is about 1.14 days: an exceedance is one disturbed day, occasionally two, not a siege, which is why the expected 1.42 such days per mission is survivable.

## Picture it

Think of storms that cut the power: what matters is not only how often they come but how long each cut lasts. A one-day cut is a nuisance; a week-long one is a different house. Here the cuts are runs of days above the design level, and at G3 they last about 1.14 days: six of one day and one of two.

## Where it breaks

At G3 it is an average of only seven events, six of one day and one of two, so the decimals mean little. It counts whole-day averages, so a violent six hours can fail to show at all, and any quieter day ends a run, which is a choice rather than physics.

## Common misreading

It is easy to read 1.14 as a precise dwell time. It is 8 days over 7 runs, and taking out the single two-day run makes it exactly 1. It is also measured on daily averages, so a storm that peaks for six hours may never count at all; the event here is coarser than the one a spacecraft feels.
