## Said simply

The geomagnetic disturbance the design commits to keep operating through on a single day: a daily level of 132, the published threshold for a 'strong' storm. It sits between the sustained commitment of 48 and the survival commitment of 207.

## Picture it

Think of a ladder with three rungs: 48 for working indefinitely, 132 for working through one rough day, and 207 for the one storm that must not destroy the vehicle. This row is the middle rung. A design whose three rungs were the same height would not have been thought about.

## Where it breaks

The sheet's own note still calls this a survival level while its question says operating; the header says the question is the settled meaning. The threshold is checkable but not proven right: where a design must stop depends on thermal, torque and propellant limits this subsystem does not see.

## Common misreading

Collapsing this into the storm requirement. The worst day of an ordinary design band and the one storm of the mission are different questions.
