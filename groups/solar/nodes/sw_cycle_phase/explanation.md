How far through its roughly eleven-year solar cycle the mission starts, from zero at the start of the cycle to one at its end.

## Said simply

How far through its roughly eleven-year solar cycle the mission starts, from zero at the start of the cycle to one at its end. At the declared start it is 0.619, a little past the peak on the declining side, which is the storm-rich part of the cycle.

## Picture it

Think of a clock whose hand goes round once per solar cycle, about eleven years. Midnight is the cycle's start; activity climbs to its top a little before halfway round and then takes longer to fall. At the mission's declared start the hand reads 0.619: past the top, on the way down.

## Where it breaks

The current cycle has not finished, so its length is taken as the mean of the two complete cycles, 11.44 years; if this cycle runs longer, the phase is too large. The phase is also a fraction of time, not of activity, because the cycle rises faster than it falls.

## Common misreading

A phase of 0.619 is not 61.9 per cent of any activity level. Equal steps in phase are not equal steps in activity, because the cycle climbs quickly and takes about twice as long to fall.
