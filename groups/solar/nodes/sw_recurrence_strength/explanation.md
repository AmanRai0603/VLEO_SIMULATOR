## Said simply

How strongly the Sun's flux one rotation ahead echoes today's, once the eleven-year cycle is removed: a correlation of 0.376, which accounts for about 14 per cent, one seventh, of the variation. ## Where it breaks

It is one number pooled over cycles 23, 24 and the rise of 25, but the echo differs across a cycle: at maximum many active regions blur it, near minimum one long-lived region can dominate it. It is also only the first peak; the echo continues faintly at the second and third rotations.

## Common misreading

That a correlation of 0.376 is the share of the signal that recurs. The share explained is the correlation squared, 0.1414, so quoting the correlation overstates the recurrence by nearly three times.
