## Said simply

The solar flux the average solar cycle shows at a given point in the cycle, found by stacking the record's complete cycles on top of each other and averaging. At the declared mission start, 0.6194 of the way through the cycle, it gives 108.14 solar flux units against the record's plain average of 114.84: that 6.7 is what knowing the date buys.

## Where it breaks

It is a mean, so half the days at any point in the cycle are above it; it is a centre for a design value, not a design value. It averages only two complete cycles, which peaked near 196 and 146, and matches neither.

## Common misreading

That a point in the cycle stands in for a level of activity. The average cycle rises fast and falls slowly, so two 'mid-cycle' points, 0.2 and 0.8, are owed 111 and 76 solar flux units.
