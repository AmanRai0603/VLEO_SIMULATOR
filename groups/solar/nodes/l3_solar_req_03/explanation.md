## Said simply

The geomagnetic storm the design must not be destroyed by: a daily disturbance level of 207, the published threshold for a 'severe' storm. It is separate from the lower level of 132 the vehicle keeps operating through.

## Picture it

Think of a building with two earthquake ratings: the shaking it stays in use through, and the stronger shaking in which it may be closed but must not collapse. For this vehicle the first is 132 and the second is this row, 207. A storm in between is survived, not worked through.

## Where it breaks

The record expects 158.4 over five years, so the mission will meet a storm it cannot work through: about 1.42 days over the mission, in one or two one-day events. A daily figure also blurs a violent six-hour storm and a mild day-long one, and the record is only 29 years long.

## Common misreading

Taking 207 as the level the vehicle operates through. It is a survival commitment; the operating level is 132 and did not move.
