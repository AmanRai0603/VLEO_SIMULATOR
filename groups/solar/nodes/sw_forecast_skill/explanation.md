## Where it breaks

Beating the simple guess is not the same as being accurate: even at its best the outlook's typical error is about 18 per cent of a typical flux. It is one score over 29 years, and the simple guess is strong when the Sun is quiet and weak in a rising cycle.

## Common misreading

A positive score is easy to read as a good forecast. It only means the outlook's error grows more slowly than the simple guess's; the forecast is never accurate in this window.
