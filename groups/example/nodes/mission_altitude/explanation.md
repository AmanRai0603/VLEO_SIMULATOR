## In one line

The mission flies at 400 km: a choice, not a calculation.

## Said simply

Every other number in this group starts here. The altitude is chosen by the mission — low enough to see the ground well, high enough that the thin air does not bring the spacecraft down too soon.

## Where it breaks

A real orbit's altitude is not one number: it changes around the orbit and falls slowly as drag acts. This node is the design altitude, the value everything is sized for.

## Common misreading

"Altitude" here is height above the Earth's equatorial radius, not above the ground directly beneath — over a mountain or near a pole the two differ by kilometres.
