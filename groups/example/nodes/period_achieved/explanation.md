## In one line

The design achieves a 92.6-minute orbit, under the 95-minute limit by 2.4 minutes.

## Said simply

This is the period the design actually gives, set beside the limit so the requirement can be read at a glance.

## Guess first

{{guess If the mission raised its altitude to 600 km, would it still meet the limit? || No. A 600 km orbit takes about 96.7 minutes, over the limit. The highest altitude that meets 95 minutes is about 520 km.}}

## Where it breaks

It carries the period of the design altitude only; a mission that changes altitude over its life has to meet the limit at the highest one.

## Common misreading

Thinking the achieved value is chosen. It is not: it comes from {{node orbit_period}}, which comes from the altitude.
