## In one line

One orbit may take no more than 95 minutes.

## Said simply

The ground station has to see the spacecraft often enough to download its pictures. The mission worked out that an orbit longer than 95 minutes leaves gaps it cannot accept, so 95 minutes is the limit.

## Where it breaks

The real constraint is the time between passes over the ground station, which depends on the Earth's rotation too. The period is a stand-in for it in this example.

## Common misreading

Reading the limit as a target to reach rather than a ceiling to stay under. It binds with `<=`: the achieved period must stay under it.
