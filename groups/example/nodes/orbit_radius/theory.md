## Equations

$$r = R_{\oplus} + h$$

## Derivation

The radius is the sum of the Earth's equatorial radius and the altitude above it, both in metres.

## Assumptions

- $R_{\oplus} = 6378137\ \mathrm{m}$, the WGS-84 equatorial radius.
- A negative altitude is below the surface and is refused.

## Validity

For every altitude from 150 km to 450 km.
