## In one line

The spacecraft at 400 km is 6,778 km from the centre of the Earth — the distance gravity actually cares about.

## Said simply

Gravity pulls toward the centre of the Earth, not toward the ground. So before anything else, the altitude is turned into the distance from the centre: add the Earth's radius.

## Where it breaks

The Earth bulges at the equator by about 21 km, so the true distance depends on latitude. The equatorial radius is used throughout this group.

## Common misreading

Treating altitude as if it were the radius — using 400 km where 6,778 km belongs — gives speeds more than four times too high.
