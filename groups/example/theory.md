## Equations

The group's nodes are chained by one relation between speed and period, {{eq G1}}, which follows from a circle of radius $r$ travelled at constant speed $v$.

## Derivation

1. A circular orbit of radius $r$ has circumference $2\pi r$.
2. Travelled at constant speed $v$, it takes $T = 2\pi r / v$.
3. With the circular speed $v = \sqrt{\mu / r}$ from {{node orbit_speed}}, $T = 2\pi \sqrt{r^{3}/\mu}$.

## Assumptions

- Two bodies only: the Earth as a point mass $\mu$, the spacecraft's mass negligible.
- The orbit is exactly circular: eccentricity $e = 0$.
- No drag, no thrust, no third body.

## Validity

Holds while drag and the Earth's oblateness ($J_2$) change $r$ by much less than a percent per orbit: above roughly 300 km for the speed, and for the period over a few orbits at any altitude in the range.
