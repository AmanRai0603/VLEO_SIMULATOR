3Where the simple version breaks

An average hides any peaks, and the sheet does not say how the draw varies around the orbit. It is only held to be sensible up to 3 kilowatts, above which the payload is the spacecraft.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside 0 … 3000 W the row refuses rather than answers: a payload load cannot be negative; above 3 kW the payload is the spacecraft.

4Common wrong idea

It is easy to read this as the payload's draw while working. It is the draw averaged over a whole orbit.
