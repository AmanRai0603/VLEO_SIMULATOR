2Now the real thing

How fast does the accelerated beam leave the thruster?

v_e = sqrt(2*q*V_b/m_i) sourced · romano2021

Source romano2021 — Romano, F. et al. — Intake design for an atmosphere-breathing electric propulsion system Acta Astronautica, 2021

Air is lighter than xenon, so the same voltage buys a far higher exhaust velocity. It is the one respect in which air-breathing propulsion is easier rather than harder.

3Where the simple version breaks

It takes every ion as singly charged and of the air's average mass, so a mixed beam is described by one average. Below 1 kilometre per second the concept has no advantage over a cold gas thruster; above 500 kilometres per second the relativistic and space-charge assumptions in the relation break down.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside 1000 … 500000 m/s the row refuses rather than answers: below 1 km/s the exhaust is slower than a cold gas thruster and the concept has no advantage; above 500 km/s the relativistic and space-charge assumptions in the relation break down.
