What is the drag coefficient of this body, at this speed ratio, with this surface?

Cd = P/sqrt(pi) + gamma*Q*Z + (gamma/2)*vr*(gamma*sqrt(pi)*Z + P)

Source doornbos2011 — Doornbos, E. — Thermospheric density and wind determination from satellite dynamics TU Delft, 2011

Treating this as a constant 2.2 is the single largest avoidable error in a VLEO drag estimate, and it is what most concept studies do.

Assumptions

Free-molecular flow — a molecule leaving the surface never returns and never strikes an incoming one
