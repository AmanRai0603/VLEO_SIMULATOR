3Where the simple version breaks

The only propellant it adds is the disposal propellant, so any other propellant carried is not in this sum. Below a kilogram there is no spacecraft; above 6 tonnes the vehicle is not in this class.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.
