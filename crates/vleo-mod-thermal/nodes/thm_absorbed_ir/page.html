3Where the simple version breaks

It applies the Earth's mean outgoing heat radiation to the spacecraft's frontal area, so it is one average figure rather than the load on a particular surface. The sheet states no assumptions and no derivation.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside 0 … 100000 W the row refuses rather than answers: absorbed power cannot be negative; above 100 kW the surface is not this spacecraft.

4Common wrong idea

That a low-orbiting spacecraft gets as cold in eclipse as intuition from higher orbits suggests. The Earth's heat radiation keeps arriving in eclipse, and it stops that happening.
