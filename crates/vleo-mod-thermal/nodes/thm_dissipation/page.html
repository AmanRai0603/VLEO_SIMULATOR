3Where the simple version breaks

It ignores the small share of power stored in the battery over a cycle, which averages to zero over an orbit, so it is an orbit-average figure. The sheet gives no derivation.

Assumptions, and where each stops holding
All bus power that is not beam kinetic energy or radiated radio power becomes heat
fails when true to within the accuracy of this budget; it ignores the small fraction stored in the battery over a cycle, which averages to zero over an orbit

Outside 0 … 50000 W the row refuses rather than answers: heat to reject cannot be negative; above 50 kW nothing in this mass class dissipates that much.

4Common wrong idea

That all the power drawn becomes heat. The thruster beam's kinetic power and the radiated radio power leave the spacecraft and are taken off.
