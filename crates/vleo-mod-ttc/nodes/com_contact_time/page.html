3Where the simple version breaks

It assumes the pass is directly overhead. The mean over many passes is roughly 60 per cent of this, so a downlink volume computed from it will not be achieved.

4Common wrong idea

That every pass lasts this long. This is the overhead pass; averaged over many passes the contact is roughly 60 per cent of it.
