3Where the simple version breaks

It is only as good as the weight per square metre it is given, and it takes the array's mass to grow in step with its area. Above a tonne the array is not part of this spacecraft, so an answer there means the inputs are wrong.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside 0 … 1000 kg the row refuses rather than answers: a mass cannot be negative; above a tonne the array is not part of this spacecraft.
