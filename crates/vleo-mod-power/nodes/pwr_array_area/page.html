1Say it simply

How big the unfolded solar array is: 6 square metres. It is chosen by hand on purpose, because a bigger array gives more power but also more drag, and sizing it from power alone would hide that trade. It feeds the power margin.

3Where the simple version breaks

It is not worked out from the power the spacecraft needs. Below half a square metre no useful power comes out at any efficiency, and above 40 square metres the array's own drag exceeds the thrust the intake can produce at every altitude in the band.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside 0.5 … 40 m^2 the row refuses rather than answers: below 0.5 m2 no useful power is produced at any efficiency; above 40 m2 the array's own drag exceeds the thrust the intake can produce at every altitude in the band.

4Common wrong idea

It is tempting to think the array should simply be sized to meet the power demand; here it is deliberately a design choice, because its size also drives drag.
