3Where the simple version breaks

It is one average angle for the whole orbit, not the angle at any moment. Zero is the best case of light striking straight on, and above 85 degrees almost nothing is collected and the array is not pointed at the Sun in any useful sense.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside 0 … 85 deg the row refuses rather than answers: zero is normal incidence, the best case and the limit; above 85 degrees the cosine loss leaves almost nothing and the array is not pointed at the Sun in any useful sense.
