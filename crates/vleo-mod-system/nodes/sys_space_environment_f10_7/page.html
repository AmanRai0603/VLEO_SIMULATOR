3Where the simple version breaks

None of the subsystem's caveats come with it: the value stacks two one-sided allowances, so the day is nearer one in a hundred than one in twenty, and the band behind it is the 90th percentile while the run is labelled 95 per cent. The sheet describes it both as the sustained level and as the single hot day, reading 124.14.
