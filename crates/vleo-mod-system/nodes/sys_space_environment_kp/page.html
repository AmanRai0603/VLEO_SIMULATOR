What Kp does the system design to, in the worst three-hour slot of its design day?

Kp_sys = l3_solar_interface.kp_peak_hotmean

Source iaga_kp_ap — Bartels, J. — The standardized index Ks and the planetary index Kp, with the ap equivalent-amplitude table IATME Bulletin 12b, IUGG Publication Office, 1949; the 28-point table as published by IAGA and distributed by GFZ Potsdam

The peak slot and not the daily mean, and the distinction is the whole reason the subsystem measures a slot offset at all. Ap is a daily mean, Kp is reported in eight three-hourly slots, and the published conversion run on a daily mean lands ABOVE the mean of the eight and far BELOW the peak. A design that converts Ap to Kp and stops is sized on a sky quieter than the record's, on exactly the days a drag design is sized by.

A layer-2 row states what the SYSTEM works with and computes nothing. This one receives the third of the three drivers a thermospheric density model takes, alongside sys_space_environment_f10_7 and sys_space_environment_f10_7_81day.

Which member is the content of the row. The crossing carries ten Kp values — two slots across five scenarios — and they span 1.27 to 8.00, which is the difference between a quiet day and a severe storm. Naming the wrong one changes the atmosphere this system designs to, and nothing in the tree would notice: all ten are dimensionless, in the same declared domain, produced by one node.

Reading the answer

7.9966 at the declared window: the worst three hours of the disturbed design day, which is a G4 severe storm.

That number is high and it follows from a correction rather than from a judgement. The subsystem's hot Ap day is 90.55, where the study this was ported from publishes 41.54, because the within-rotation band is conditioned on the rotation level; the peak-slot offset at that Ap is 1.75 Kp on top of a converted 6.24. The study's own equivalent is Kp 6.14.

For a reader who wants the ordinary day rather than the worst slot of the worst one, the crossing carries nine other Kp members and each is one input away.
