explanation

1Say it simply

Kp is a scale from 0 to 9 of how disturbed Earth's magnetic field is. This is the Kp the system designs to in the worst three hours of its design day, the third input an upper-atmosphere density model takes. The worst three hours matter because converting the daily average alone reads a quieter sky than the record shows.

In plain words by an assistant, from what this sheet states — for its owner (environment) to confirm.

2Now the real thing

What Kp does the system design to, in the worst three-hour slot of its design day?

Kp_sys = l3_solar_interface.kp_peak_hotmean sourced · iaga_kp_ap

Source iaga_kp_ap — Bartels, J. — The standardized index Ks and the planetary index Kp, with the ap equivalent-amplitude table IATME Bulletin 12b, IUGG Publication Office, 1949; the 28-point table as published by IAGA and distributed by GFZ Potsdam

The peak slot and not the daily mean, and the distinction is the whole reason the subsystem measures a slot offset at all. Ap is a daily mean, Kp is reported in eight three-hourly slots, and the published conversion run on a daily mean lands ABOVE the mean of the eight and far BELOW the peak. A design that converts Ap to Kp and stops is sized on a sky quieter than the record's, on exactly the days a drag design is sized by.

3Where the simple version breaks

The worst-slot correction is a median, so half the days at the same activity have a worse peak. At the declared window it reads 7.9966, a severe storm, following from a corrected daily activity of 90.55; the study it came from gives 6.14. The sheet's relation names the sustained scenario while its explanation names the disturbed single day.

4Common wrong idea

That converting daily activity to Kp and stopping there is enough. The conversion lands above the day's average slot and far below its peak, so a design sized on it alone meets a quieter sky than the record's.
