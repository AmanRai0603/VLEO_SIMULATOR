Reading the answer

104.07 sfu at the declared window. Two things about that number are worth a system reader's attention and neither is visible from here.

It is far below the 158.33 the study this was ported from publishes for its own window, because sw_central_expectation reads a cycle analogue over the mission's own dates rather than holding the last rotation forecast forward. That is a recorded decision, not a discrepancy.

And it is a 1.28-sigma band edge, which is the 90th percentile although the run is labelled 95 per cent. The credibility vector travels across the seam; that does not.

THE 81-DAY MEAN NOW ARRIVES TOO, as sys_space_environment_f10_7_81day, which was added after step 6 found it missing. Every thermospheric density model in use takes the pair, daily and 81-day, because the two carry different physics: the daily value drives the EUV heating of the day and the 81-day mean the background state. It carries the same number this row does, 104.07, because the hot day's 81-day companion IS the hot sustained level.

WHAT STILL DOES NOT ARRIVE is Kp, which does not cross at all. §20.8 names the three ways out and none has been chosen. It is recorded on sys_space_environment_atmospheric_density, which is where somebody writing that row will meet it.
