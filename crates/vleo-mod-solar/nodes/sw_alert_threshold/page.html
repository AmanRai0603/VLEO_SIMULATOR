3Where the simple version breaks

Level 4 is 'active', one step below the storm scale, and the record reaches it on about a third of all days, so treating every alert as a storm would mean reacting a third of the time. It is what one centre alerted on over 29 years, not what a given spacecraft should react to.

explanation

Every other row in this subsystem measures the sky. This one measures the people watching it, and that is the point: a mission's own alerting should not sit below the level at which the public service already reacts, because below that level a mission would be responding to activity nobody is warning about. So the number is READ from the thresholds attached to the alerts as issued, rather than chosen here — which makes it a fact about operational practice over 29 years rather than an engineering judgement.

Each line below is derived from the one before, ending at the relation — which is sourced · noaa_swpc.

  1. The alerts as issued carry the threshold that triggered them, so the question is answerable by reading a column rather than by inference. First the population has to be identified: of the alerts in the record, 9408 carry a K threshold, 119 carry an A threshold, and 8444 carry none at all — those are summaries, cancellations and electron-flux notices.
  2. The A-threshold alerts are a different quantity and are deliberately not in this row. They are daily-index alerts at values 20, 30 and 50; this row is the three-hourly K one, because a node answers one question.
  3. Among the K alerts the thresholds used are 4 through 9, and their counts fall steeply — which is itself a check that the column is being read correctly, since alerts must become rarer as the trigger rises.K 4: 5262 5: 2953 6: 901 7: 233 8: 38 9: 3
  4. The answer wanted is the FLOOR, because the question is where watching begins rather than where it intensifies. So the relation is the minimum of the legitimate values.K_alert = min = 4
  5. Taking the minimum also happens to make this row immune to a defect in the source that nothing in the bundle marks. Alongside the K values sit eleven parsed thresholds of 414, 413, 425, 419, 430 and 408 — a parser running two numbers together, not a Kp of 414. The minimum ignores them; any relation taking a maximum or a mean of that column would be wrong.
