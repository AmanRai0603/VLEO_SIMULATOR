What F10.7 does the average solar cycle show at this phase?

F107_cyc(phase) = mean over complete cycles of F10.7 at that phase

Source noaa_swpc — NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center — solar and geomagnetic indices NOAA, retrieved 2026-09-01

The climatology that knows where in the cycle it is. sw_central_expectation currently hands over to the record's unconditional mean of 114.8 sfu because no date was available; at the declared epoch's phase of 0.619 this row says 105.8 instead. That difference is what having a date buys.

Assumptions

Guards

  • F107_cyc ≥ 60 — the same floor as env_f107: below 60 sfu has never been observed and no relation reading F10.7 has support there
  • F107_cyc ≤ 400 — the same ceiling as env_f107: above 400 sfu every consumer of F10.7 is extrapolating. The largest binned mean is 165.3, so this bound is unreachable by the relation and catches a broken table
