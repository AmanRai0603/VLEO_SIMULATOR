How cold can a single day of F10.7 be inside the mission window?

F107_cold_short = F107_cold_long - dF107_day_low

Source noaa_swpc — NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center — solar and geomagnetic indices NOAA, retrieved 2026-09-01

The coldest of the five scenarios, and the one a drag-based capability is weakest at. Its hot twin, sw_f107_design_short, is the worst single day upward; this is the worst single day downward, and the two are NOT mirror images even though the rows look identical bar a sign. They are not mirrors because the two terms behave differently. The rotation band is symmetric — both edges are 1.28 standard deviations from the centre. The daily term is not: 34.23 sfu up against 31.27 down. So the hot day sits further above nominal than the cold day sits below it, by about three sfu, and that asymmetry is the record's shape rather than a rounding artefact.

The two spreads are of different things and they stack rather than combine. sw_mean_band_spread is how wrong the pattern is about the LEVEL a rotation sits at — a forecast error. sw_daily_band_drop is how far a day falls below the rotation it belongs to — the sun's own variability, measured on days that already happened. A day is low because its rotation is low AND because it is a low day within that rotation, so the design level for a cold day is the sustained cold level with the daily drop taken off.

That is a deliberately conservative construction and the sheet says so. Stacking two one-sided percentiles gives a bound further out than either — roughly the 99th rather than the 95th if the two were independent and normal — so the published day is rarer than the confidence attached to it suggests. The study does the same thing and the port follows it.

The one term that differs from the hot twin is which tail of the daily sample is used, and it is a different NUMBER rather than the same number negated: sw_daily_band_drop is 31.27 where sw_daily_band_spread is 34.23. Reading the hot row and flipping a sign would make this scenario three sfu colder than the record supports, which is the specific error this row exists to make impossible.

  1. Take the sustained cold level: the centre less 1.28 standard deviations of the rotation-forecast residual.F107_cold_long = sw_f107_cold_long
  2. Take how far a day falls below its own rotation, at the declared confidence. The LOW tail, which is not the high tail negated: 31.27 against 34.23.dF107_day_low = sw_daily_band_drop = 31.2722 sfu
  3. Subtract it. The two are stacked rather than combined in quadrature, which is what the study does and is conservative — a stacked pair of one-sided percentiles is a rarer day than either confidence describes.F107_cold_short = F107_cold_long - dF107_day_low

Reading the answer

Fed the study's own two numbers the arithmetic is exact: its coldmean 141.0544028132 minus its own daily drop of 31.3333333333 is 109.7210694798, which is the coldday scenario it published to the last digit. With this repository's own two terms it is 109.8366, 0.11 per cent away. That is what the parity grid beside this sheet checks, and it is a check on the RELATION.

THIS ROW REFUSES ON THE NOMINAL CASE, AND THAT IS THE FINDING. Its own chain does not reach 109. sw_central_expectation reads the cycle analogue over the mission's own dates rather than holding the last rotation forecast forward, and at the declared epoch it gives 86.8497 sfu — a deliberate and recorded departure from the study's 158.33. Carry 86.8497 down this chain and the sustained cold level is 69.6281 and the cold DAY is 38.3559, which is below the guard at 60, so the row returns OutOfDomain rather than a number. `vleo run sw_f107_cold_short` prints exactly that, and names this row as the one blocked.

That is the right behaviour and it should not be traded away. 38 sfu has never been observed; the quiet sun's 10.7 cm floor is near 64, and the record's own minimum is above 60. The guard is not too tight — the construction is wrong at this centre. Stacking a symmetric 1.28-sigma band on a 5th-percentile daily drop takes 48.5 sfu off an 86.8 sfu sun, more than half of it, and a sun cannot lose more than half of itself. The crossing is at a centre of 108.5: any case whose centre is below that refuses here.

The three things that could resolve it are all upstream of this row and none is a change to it. A sigma measured for the window's own phase rather than pooled across the cycle would be narrower near minimum. Percentiles of the residuals in place of a normal multiplier would not assume a symmetry the sample does not have. And the daily drop applies to a day inside a rotation, not to a rotation already at the low edge of its own band — stacking the two is the conservative choice the study made, and at a low centre conservative stops meaning safe and starts meaning impossible.
