What daily Ap is this design built to survive?

Ap_design(G) = ap at the top Kp of that G band: G1 -> 48, G2 -> 80, G3 -> 132

Source iaga_kp_ap — Bartels, J. — The standardized index Ks and the planetary index Kp, with the ap equivalent-amplitude table IATME Bulletin 12b, IUGG Publication Office, 1949; the 28-point table as published by IAGA and distributed by GFZ Potsdam

The design-side companion to sw_storm_return_level. That row says what the record will present over the mission; this says what the vehicle is built for, from the G level sw_storm_design_level declares. At the default G3 the answer is 132 and the record's five-year expectation is 158.4, and the gap between them is the finding.

Assumptions

Guards

  • Ap_design ≥ 40 — the lowest value this relation can return is 48, at G1. A bound at 40 sits just under it and refuses anything that would design to less than a minor storm, which is not a design case: the record has 1358 days at G1 or above, 47 a year
  • Ap_design ≤ 140 — the highest this relation can return is 132, at G3, which is the top of the declared G range. A bound at 140 sits just above it and catches a G level outside the range or a misread table. It does NOT bound the sky: the record's largest daily Ap is 273 and the five-year return level is 158.4, both above this bound, and neither passes through this row

The reason travels with the guard. A guard whose reason is not written down gets deleted by the next person who finds it awkward.
