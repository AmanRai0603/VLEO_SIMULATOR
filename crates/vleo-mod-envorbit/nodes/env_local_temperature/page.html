2Now the real thing

What is the gas temperature at the flight altitude?

T(z) = T_inf - (T_inf - T_120)*exp(-s*(z - z_120)) sourced · jacchia1971

Source jacchia1971 — Jacchia, L. G. — Revised static models of the thermosphere and exosphere with empirical temperature profiles SAO Special Report 332, 1971

3Where the simple version breaks

It is a smooth profile between two ends, the 120 kilometre base and the limiting upper-atmosphere temperature, and nothing else. Below the base temperature the profile is not defined, and it cannot exceed the upper-atmosphere temperature it approaches.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside 200 … 2500 K the row refuses rather than answers: below the 120 km base temperature the profile is not defined; cannot exceed the exospheric temperature it approaches.
