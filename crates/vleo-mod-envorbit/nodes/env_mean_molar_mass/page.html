3Where the simple version breaks

It is a mass-weighted average over the five gases the model carries. The sheet holds it between 2 grams per mole, which would be pure hydrogen, and 30, heavier than molecular nitrogen; neither occurs in this band.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside 0.002 … 0.03 kg/mol the row refuses rather than answers: below 2 g/mol the mixture would be pure hydrogen, which does not occur in this band; above 30 g/mol the mixture would be heavier than molecular nitrogen, which does not occur above the base.
