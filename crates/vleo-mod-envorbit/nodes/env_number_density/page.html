n = SUM_i n_i(z) sourced · jacchia1971

Source jacchia1971 — Jacchia, L. G. — Revised static models of the thermosphere and exosphere with empirical temperature profiles SAO Special Report 332, 1971

3Where the simple version breaks

It is a sum of each gas settled by its own weight as if undisturbed, driven only by altitude and the upper-atmosphere temperature. Below a hundred million per cubic metre there is nothing for an intake to collect; above a hundred billion billion the flow is continuous and the intake model does not apply.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside 100000000 … 100000000000000000000 1/m^3 the row refuses rather than answers: below 1e8 per cubic metre there is nothing for an intake to collect; above 1e20 the flow is continuum and the intake model does not apply.
