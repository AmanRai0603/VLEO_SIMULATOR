3Where the simple version breaks

It assumes uniform coverage of the whole globe with no weighting by latitude. A Sun-synchronous constellation revisits the poles far more often than the equator, so this mean is optimistic at low latitudes and pessimistic at high ones.

Assumptions, and where each stops holding

Outside 0 … 2400 h the row refuses rather than answers: a revisit time cannot be negative; above 100 days the constellation does not provide a service.

4Common wrong idea

That the mean revisit is the revisit at any particular place. It is a global average: at low latitudes the real revisit is longer than this, and at high latitudes shorter.
