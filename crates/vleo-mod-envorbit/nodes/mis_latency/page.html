3Where the simple version breaks

It adds four times end to end, so it is only as good as each of them and knows nothing the four do not carry. Above two days the product is not the near-real-time one being sold.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside 0 … 2880 min the row refuses rather than answers: a latency cannot be negative; above two days the product is not the near-real-time one being sold.

4Common wrong idea

That latency is mostly the downlink or the processing. The wait for the next downlink dominates, and it is the part a constellation design can actually move.
