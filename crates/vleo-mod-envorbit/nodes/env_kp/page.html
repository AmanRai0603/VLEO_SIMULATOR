It restates what the system layer was told; it does not decide it.

Kp indexes the energy the auroral electrojets deposit directly into the thermosphere, and what level a design should be sized to over a mission window is a statement about that window — the subsystem's work, not this row's. It was declared here as 3 while the subsystem computed 5.198 over the same window, and nothing compared them.

Reading the answer

It is QUASI-LOGARITHMIC and it is a slot. Kp is not a linear index, which is why the relation reading it has an exponential term as well as a linear one, and it is not a single daily number either — the record has eight three-hourly values a day and a daily statistic has to choose between their mean and their peak.

The value rose from 3 to 5.198 when this wiring landed, which through the thermosphere relation is a substantially hotter and therefore denser upper atmosphere. That direction is the opposite of what env_f107 did, and both are the record's own answer replacing a round number.
