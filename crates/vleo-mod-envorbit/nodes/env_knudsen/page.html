1Say it simply

How far a gas molecule travels between collisions compared with the size of the vehicle. When that distance is far larger, the air meets the spacecraft as separate molecules rather than as a flowing fluid, which is called free-molecular flow, and that is what licenses every aerodynamic relation in this tree.

3Where the simple version breaks

It uses one characteristic length for the whole vehicle. Below ten the flow is transitional and the free-molecular relations are outside their envelope; that bound is the Ariane 501 lesson written as a guard, against a component used outside the envelope it was specified for. Above a million million the number is meaningless but harmless.
