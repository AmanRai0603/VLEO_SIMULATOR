1Say it simply

Whether the power system supplies what the spacecraft asks for: how far the power available exceeds or falls short of the power demanded, as a signed fraction of the demand. A negative answer is a shortfall, and it is reported as one.

2Now the real thing

Does the power system supply what the spacecraft asks for?

M = (P_avail - P_dem)/P_dem sourced · ecss_e_st_10_02

Source ecss_e_st_10_02 — ECSS-E-ST-10-02C — Verification ESA-ESTEC, 2018

A margin silently corrected to zero is a design that drifted without anyone deciding to. This one is signed.

3Where the simple version breaks

It compares two single figures, available and demanded power, so it is only as good as they are. The sheet treats a margin below minus one hundred per cent as no longer a design, and one above ten thousand per cent as an absurdly oversized array.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside -1 … 100 the row refuses rather than answers: a margin below -100% would mean the demand is more than twice the supply and the design is not a design; above 10000% the array is absurdly oversized.

4Common wrong idea

That a negative power margin should be tidied up to zero. A margin silently corrected to zero is a design that drifted without anyone deciding to, so this one is signed and never clamped.
