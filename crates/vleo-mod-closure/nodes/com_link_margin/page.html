Source ecss_e_st_50 — ECSS-E-ST-50-05C — Radio frequency and modulation ESA-ESTEC, 2011

3Where the simple version breaks

It is only as good as the three numbers it combines: what the link delivers, what is required, and the implementation loss. Below minus 30 decibels the design is wrong somewhere upstream; above 40 decibels the link is overdesigned by orders of magnitude.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside -30 … 40 dB the row refuses rather than answers: below -30 dB the link is not close to closing and the design is wrong somewhere upstream; above 40 dB the link is overdesigned by orders of magnitude.
