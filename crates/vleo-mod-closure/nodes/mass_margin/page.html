1Say it simply

Whether the spacecraft fits within the mass it is allowed: how much of the mass limit is left once the fully fuelled spacecraft is counted, as a signed fraction of that limit. A negative answer means the spacecraft is too heavy.

2Now the real thing

Does the spacecraft fit the mass it is allowed?

M = (m_lim - m_wet)/m_lim sourced · ecss_e_st_10_02

Source ecss_e_st_10_02 — ECSS-E-ST-10-02C — Verification ESA-ESTEC, 2018

3Where the simple version breaks

It knows only the two masses it is given, so it is as trustworthy as the mass estimate and the declared limit. A margin below minus five hundred per cent means the design is five times over and the inputs are wrong; a margin of one would mean the spacecraft weighs nothing.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.

Outside -5 … 1 the row refuses rather than answers: a margin below -500% means the design is five times over and the inputs are wrong; a margin of one means the spacecraft weighs nothing.
