3Where the simple version breaks

It carries one source of error only: the density uncertainty applied to the drag, carried over exactly one day. Above 10000 kilometres the number stops meaning a distance the spacecraft is out by; it means the model is unusable there.

No assumption stated. A multi-step relation always has at least one.
